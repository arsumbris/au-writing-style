The reader may stop after the first line.
Put the point in that line.

Cut the words that only delay the point:
- The summary that repeats what is below
- The "this matters because" that tells the reader how to feel
- The "maybe" or "probably" that the note already shows
- Any wind-up before the point

The title and the description already frame the note.
Write the point in the body.
