type: writing-rule
---

Put a line break after every sentence.
Each sentence stands on its own line.

Give each fact its own line too.
A sentence that joins two thoughts with "and" or "so" holds two facts.
When a line holds two facts, break it in two.

A block of sentences hides where one ends and the next starts.
A line each lets the reader find, move and cut any line on its own.
