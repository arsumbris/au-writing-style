type: writing-rule
---

Every word you keep is work for later.
Someone reads it again and checks that it is still true.

So keep few words.
Keep what the truth needs and no more.

Cut a word that does no work.
Do not cut a real point.

Test each sentence.
Does it help the reader do one of these:
- Decide
- Build
- Remember
- Check
- Understand

Cut the sentence that helps with none of them.
Any real help counts, not only these five.
