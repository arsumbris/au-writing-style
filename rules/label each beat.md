A multi-beat body needs a label on each beat.
The label chunks the body for a scanner.

Size the label to the beat:
- A long beat, a study or a spec section, takes a heading
- A medium beat, a proposal section, takes a bold lead-in
- A short beat, a finding or a receipt, takes no label

A short beat and the first beat need no label.
Their first line carries the point.

To check, the labels alone map the body.
