A flourish is a sentence shaped for effect rather than the point.

Common flourishes:
- The false contrast, "It's not X. It's Y."
- The fake-insight setup, "What nobody tells you"
- The grand kicker, a closing line that sounds deep and says nothing
- The recap that repeats what the reader just read
- Any shape that sounds deep and adds nothing

The shape hides a thin or missing point.
State the point plainly or cut the sentence.
"It's not the model, it's the eval" becomes "the eval matters more than the model".
