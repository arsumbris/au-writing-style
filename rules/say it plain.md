Write for a smart coworker, not to sound clever.

Use the plain choice every time:
- The short word, not the long one
- The plain word, not the jargon
- The active voice, not the passive
- The positive statement, not the negative
- The short sentence, not the long one

A long word chosen to sound smart means the same as the short one.
Use the short one.

Keep the precise term when no plain word carries the meaning.
Define it once.
Reuse it.
