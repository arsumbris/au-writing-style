type: writing-rule
---

Push detail down under a summary line, not across as more top-level lines.
A reader scans the top level.
They drill into the branch they need.

Each parent line stands for its children.
A reader who trusts a parent skips its whole subtree.

Prefer a deep, narrow tree to a wide, flat list.
Each level stays inside the few items a reader holds.

To check, the top level alone reads as a summary.
