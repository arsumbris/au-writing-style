A hidden verb is a verb turned into a noun.
The noun form is longer.
It needs a weak helper verb like "make" or "conduct".

Turn the noun back into a verb.
"make a decision" becomes "decide".
"conduct an analysis" becomes "analyze".

Watch these endings:
- -tion
- -ment
- -sion
- -ance
- -ness

Other endings do it too.
