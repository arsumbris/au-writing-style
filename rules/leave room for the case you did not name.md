type: writing-rule
---

End a list of examples with an item that covers the rest.

Your examples never cover every case.
A list that stops at them reads as the whole class.
The reader then meets a case you left out and stops.

Put the catch-all in the list, not in the lead-in.
The reader scans the bullets and skips the line above them.

A closed set needs no catch-all.
An enum's members and a sealed family's leaves are the whole set.
Leave them closed.

Read your list.
Name a case it leaves out.
If the reader would stall there, add the item.
