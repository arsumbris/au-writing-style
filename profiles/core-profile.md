# the voice

Write so the reader parses fast enough to **steer** the work.

The reader is a smart coworker or an agent.
They **scan**, they do not read.
They hold only a few things at once.
So earn every word.

A note is a stack of **beats**.
A beat is a point and the lines under it.

Leave **air** between them.
A wall does not scan, prose or stacked lines alike.

These are defaults, not laws.
Keep the word or the case the meaning needs.

Build a note **coarse to fine**:
1. Shape the beats
2. Break the lines
3. Cut the words

The rules below follow that order.
Each is one move, written in this voice.

## shape the note

- [[start with the point:rules]]
- [[write with whitespace:rules]]
- [[label each beat:rules]]
- [[nest, don't flatten:rules]]
- [[group a long list:rules]]
- [[three or more items make a list:rules]]
- [[number a list only when ordered:rules]]
- [[leave room for the case you did not name:rules]]
- [[bold the key term once:rules]]

## then break the lines

- [[one sentence, one line:rules]]
- [[no em-dash, no semicolon:rules]]

## then cut the words

- [[say it plain:rules]]
- [[every word is debt:rules]]
- [[cut what could go anywhere:rules]]
- [[cut the flourish:rules]]
- [[cut the empty adjective:rules]]
- [[cut the optional comma:rules]]
- [[one word, one meaning:rules]]
- [[avoid the tired phrase:rules]]
- [[state the action, not the wish:rules]]
- [[kill the hidden verb:rules]]
- [[no period on a list item:rules]]
